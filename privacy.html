Privacy Policy

Your files and text never leave your browser. Optional anonymous analytics only with your consent.

Last Updated: November 6, 2026 • Effective Date: November 6, 2026

100% Client-Side Tool Processing

When you use VantorKit utilities—including PDF mergers, redactors, image converters, formatters, and calculators—all file and text processing occurs locally within your web browser using HTML5 Canvas, the Web Crypto API, and WebAssembly. Your files, photos, text contents, audio, video, and calculation inputs never leave your browser and are never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

1. Information We Collect (And What We Never Collect)

VantorKit executes all utility computations locally on your hardware. Here is how data handling is structured across the platform:

  • User Files, Text & Tool Content (Never Collected): We never receive, transmit, log, or store your uploaded images, PDFs, CSVs, audio, video, markdown documents, passwords, or tool inputs and outputs. When you close or refresh your browser tab, all temporary browser memory used by the tool is immediately released.
  • Local Storage Data: We use standard browser localStorage on your device solely to remember your preferred interface language (vantorkit_lang), your analytics consent preference (vantorkit_analytics_consent), and optional local tool drafts (such as in the Markdown Editor). This data resides on your device and is never uploaded to our servers.
  • Standard Hosting Access Logs: Like standard websites, our hosting infrastructure processes routine HTTP connection requests (such as IP addresses, browser user-agent strings, referring URLs, and timestamps) strictly to deliver static files, mitigate DDoS attacks, and maintain infrastructure stability.

2. Optional Website Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

On selected pages, VantorKit uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) (Measurement ID: G-S8MDMH082N) strictly on an opt-in basis to measure aggregate traffic and understand which tools are used. VantorKit does not serve advertisements and does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

  • Loaded Only After Your Consent: Google Analytics is blocked by default. Before you click Accept on the consent banner—or if you click Decline—zero network requests are made to Google domains (googletagmanager.com or google-analytics.com) and no analytics cookies are set.
  • What Is Collected (With Consent): When you explicitly accept analytics, GA4 collects anonymous usage events such as page views, tool page visited, device and browser type, and approximate geographic region using first-party analytics cookies (_ga and _ga_S8MDMH082N). Google Analytics 4 does not log or store full IP addresses.
  • What Is NEVER Collected: Google Analytics never receives or inspects your files, text, audio, video, passwords, tokens, calculation inputs, or any content processed inside any tool. Furthermore, privacy-sensitive tools (including PDF Redactor, JWT Decoder, Metadata Cleaner, Big-Data Transformer, CSS Grid Generator, and Video Trimmer) and legal/blog pages do not include the analytics loader at all.
  • Data Processor: Google LLC acts as the data processor for optional analytics data processed through Google Analytics 4.

How to Withdraw Consent: You can review, change, or withdraw your analytics consent at any time by clicking the Privacy settings link in the footer of the home page and tool pages that use analytics. Withdrawing consent immediately stops Google Analytics (enabling window['ga-disable-G-S8MDMH082N'] = true) and deletes the _ga cookies set by it.

3. How to Manage and Disable Cookies

You can decline or revoke analytics cookies at any time via the Privacy settings link in the site footer, or by clearing and blocking cookies in your browser settings. Declining or disabling cookies does not affect the functionality of any VantorKit tool.

4. European Privacy Rights (EEA, UK & Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you may exercise privacy rights regarding personal data, including requesting access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, or data portability, and withdrawing consent at any time.

Because VantorKit does not require user accounts and never uploads your files or tool inputs to our servers, we do not store personal user profiles or user content. For optional analytics, you may withdraw consent immediately using the Privacy settings link in the footer, or contact us at the email address below.

5. California Privacy Disclosures (Do Not Sell My Info)

For California residents, here is a factual summary of how VantorKit handles information:

  • We Do Not Sell Personal Information: VantorKit does not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties or data brokers.
  • Control & Deletion: You can revoke analytics consent and clear analytics cookies at any time via the Privacy settings link in the footer, and you may contact us to inquire about technical server log records.
  • Equal Access: All VantorKit tools provide identical features and performance regardless of whether you accept or decline optional analytics.

6. Children's Privacy

VantorKit does not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, please notify us at the address below so we can promptly delete any such records.
