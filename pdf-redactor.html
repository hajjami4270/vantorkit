Permanent PDF Redaction & Privacy Architecture

Understand how visual blackouts fail and why pixel-flattened rasterization guarantees confidentiality.

The Danger of Fake Redactions

Drawing black rectangles in Word, Preview, or Acrobat without raster flattening only creates a visual mask. The raw text stream remains intact underneath, allowing anyone to select, copy, or scrape confidential Social Security Numbers, names, and trade secrets with a simple Ctrl+C.

True Client-Side Pixel Flattening

VantorKit renders redacted pages directly to an in-memory HTML5 Canvas and burns opaque black pixels permanently over target coordinates. When exported, the original vector text objects are completely erased and replaced with flattened raster pixels, making forensic extraction mathematically impossible.

Zero-Knowledge Guarantee

Your legal briefs, financial disclosures, and medical records never leave your workstation. Rendering, redacting, signing, and PDF binary compilation execute 100% inside your browser's private memory sandbox with zero network telemetry.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about secure client-side document redaction and digital signing.

Can someone highlight or copy text hidden under the black redaction boxes?

No. When you export, pages containing redactions are rendered to high-resolution raster layers where redacted text is completely stripped from the PDF object tree and replaced with solid black pixels. There is zero underlying text stream to copy or extract.

Are my PDF documents uploaded to VantorKit servers?

Never. VantorKit operates under a zero-server architecture. All document decoding, canvas manipulation, signature placement, and PDF recompilation occur exclusively inside your device RAM.

Is the digital signature legally binding?

Yes, electronic signatures created with this tool satisfy standard e-signature criteria under frameworks like the US ESIGN Act and European eIDAS for general commercial agreements, NDAs, and routine approvals.

What is the maximum PDF file size supported?

Because all processing happens locally in browser memory, you can comfortably redact documents of dozens or hundreds of pages. The only limit is your device's available system RAM.
