Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a broken tool?

Please indicate the tool URL, your operating system, and your browser version. Because all tools run client-side, copying any red errors from your browser developer console (F12) helps us isolate and patch issues immediately.

Can I request a new utility?

Yes! We actively expand our catalog based on developer and community demand. Describe your workflow and the desired inputs and outputs.

Are my uploaded files sent to your support team?

Never. We have zero access to the files or texts you process in VantorKit. Please do not attach confidential documents in support emails.
