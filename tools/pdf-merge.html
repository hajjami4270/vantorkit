Fast, Secure & Zero-Upload PDF Merging

Learn how local browser-based PDF compilation protects your sensitive legal, medical, and personal documents from data breaches and privacy violations.

Zero Server Transmission

Traditional PDF sites upload your tax documents, contracts, and IDs to cloud servers where they may sit in temporary buckets. VantorKit processes files entirely in your browser's local sandbox memory.

Lossless Quality Preservation

Our engine copies underlying PDF content streams, embedded vector fonts, high-resolution figures, and raster graphics directly into the new container document without downsampling or degradation.

Instant Compilation Speed

Eliminate upload and download latency. Merging occurs instantaneously at the full CPU processing speed of your personal machine, making it ideal for large, multi-hundred-page binders.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions regarding local PDF merging, security, and document handling.

Is it safe to merge sensitive medical or legal documents here?

Yes. Disconnect your device from Wi-Fi or turn on Airplane mode after loading this page, and the merger will work with 100% full capability. No external HTTP request is ever made with your document data.

How do I control the order of pages in the merged file?

Documents in the list are merged in top-to-bottom sequence. You can reorder files at any time by clicking the Up (▲) and Down (▼) buttons on any item or by dragging the file card directly.

What happens to forms, bookmarks, and links in the merged PDF?

Standard visual pages and content streams are preserved. If individual source files contain form fields with conflicting internal names, they are standardized during concatenation to prevent corruption.

Are there any file size or usage limits?

There are no arbitrary daily quotas or paywalls. You can merge as many documents as your device's memory comfortably accommodates.
