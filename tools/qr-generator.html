100% Zero-Cloud Privacy

Your confidential Wi-Fi network passwords, personal contact numbers, and private messages are computed exclusively inside your local browser memory. Nothing is ever sent to or logged on any remote server.

Infinite Vector Scalability

Download lossless SVG files ready for billboards, restaurant menus, product packaging, and business cards without losing a single pixel of crispness at any print dimension.

Permanent & Direct

Generated codes are standard static matrix symbols. They do not redirect through URL shorteners or intermediate tracking domains, meaning they never expire and continue working forever.

Are my Wi-Fi credentials or vCard contacts stored anywhere?

Never. The encoding calculation takes place 100% in your device's memory. When you close the tab or change fields, the memory is cleared. No database, server endpoint, or third-party analytics collects your credentials.

Do these QR codes have a scan limit or expiration date?

No. Static QR codes encode raw text, URLs, or Wi-Fi commands directly into the visual 2D pattern. There are no scan counters, no expiration deadlines, and no monthly subscription fees.

How do phones connect to Wi-Fi using the generated QR code?

Both iOS and Android native camera apps recognize the standardized WIFI protocol (WIFI:S:...;P:...;;). When scanned, the device prompts the user to join the network with one tap without manual password entry.

Which error correction level should I select?

Level M (15%) is the industry standard balance between pattern compactness and readability. If printing on curved surfaces, rough textures, or outdoors where symbols may get scratched, choose Level Q (25%) or Level H (30%).
