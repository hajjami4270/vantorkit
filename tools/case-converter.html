1. Why URL Slugs Matter for SEO

Clean, descriptive, hyphen-separated URLs give search engines immediate context about your page content. Removing redundant stop words (like 'and', 'the', 'in') keeps URLs short and improves organic click-through rates.

2. Diacritics & Universal Normalization

Accented Latin characters (such as é, ñ, ü, ç) cause URL encoding issues (%C3%A9). Our engine normalizes Unicode via NFD to automatically convert diacritics into plain ASCII equivalents, avoiding ugly percent-encoded URLs.

3. Zero-Cloud Privacy Guarantee

Your proprietary source code, draft article titles, and confidential project names are processed 100% locally in your browser memory. Nothing is ever sent to external cloud servers or stored in remote databases.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool upload or store my text on any server?

Never. All conversions, case formats, and slug generations run strictly inside client-side JavaScript in your local browser sandbox.

What is AP / Chicago Title Case?

Title Case capitalizes all major words (nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs) while keeping minor articles and prepositions (like 'a', 'the', 'in', 'of') lowercase unless they are the first or last word of the title.

How do I generate an SEO-friendly URL slug?

Paste your article title, select your separator (hyphens '-' are recommended by Google), enable 'Remove Accents' and 'Filter Stop Words', then click 'Apply URL Slug'.

Can I chain multiple transformations?

Yes! Click the 'Swap with Input' button to transfer the converted result back into the input field and apply another transformation preset.
