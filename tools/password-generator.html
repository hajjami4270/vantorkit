Understanding Password Entropy & Cryptographic Security

How mathematical randomness protects accounts against modern offline GPU cluster attacks.

1. Cryptographic PRNG

Standard JavaScript Math.random() is pseudorandom and predictable. VantorKit uses window.crypto.getRandomValues, backed by your operating system's hardware entropy pool, eliminating predictability.

2. Shannon Entropy (E)

Entropy measures unpredictable search space: E = L * log2(R). A 16-character mixed password yields over 100 bits of entropy, requiring 2^100 brute force trials to exhaust.

3. Diceware Passphrases

Combining 4 to 6 random natural words produces passphrases that humans can easily visualize and type on mobile devices, yet remain virtually impossible for supercomputers to crack.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are generated passwords sent to any server?

No. All password generation and entropy calculations happen entirely inside your web browser using Web Crypto. No data is ever transmitted or logged.

What is the recommended password length?

For random character passwords, 16 or more characters is recommended for critical accounts. For Diceware passphrases, 4 to 5 words provides top-tier security.

How is the estimated crack time determined?

We simulate a worst-case offline attack scenario using an attacker with a high-end GPU cracking rig capable of testing 100 billion hashes per second.

Why should I avoid ambiguous characters?

Characters like capital 'O' and zero '0', or lowercase 'l' and uppercase 'I' look identical in many fonts. Excluding them makes manual entry error-free.
